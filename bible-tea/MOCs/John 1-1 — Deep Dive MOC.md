## The "Tea" (Share-Worthy Discovery)
The very first word of the Bible — *Bereshit* — hides: **Bar** (Son) + **Reshit** (Beginning) = "Son of the Beginning." The middle letters spell **Esh** (Fire). The outer letters spell **Brit** (Covenant). Genesis 1:1 encodes a **"covenant of fire" involving the Son** — and John 1:1 is the reveal.

---

## Summary
John 1:1 declares that the creative power behind Genesis 1 — the *dabar* that spoke light into existence — is not a force but a Person, who existed eternally in face-to-face communion with God while being fully God Himself. The Hebrew *Bereshit* had the Son encoded from the first syllable.
