# Genesis 1:1 — The Bereshit Connection to John 1:1

## The Echo
John 1:1 opens with **"Ἐν ἀρχῇ"** (En archē) — the exact phrase the Septuagint uses to translate **"בְּרֵאשִׁית"** (Bereshit) in Genesis 1:1.

This is John saying: *"Let me tell you what was REALLY happening in Genesis 1:1."*

## Hidden Words Inside "Bereshit" (בְּרֵאשִׁית)

### Bar (בר) = "Son"
The first two letters of *Bereshit* spell **Bar** — Aramaic/Hebrew for "Son" (as in *Bar Mitzvah*). The Bible's first word begins with "Son."

### Reshit (רֵאשִׁית) = "Beginning / Firstfruits"
The remaining letters mean "what is first" or "the choice part / firstfruits."

### Combined Reading
**Bar-Reshit** = **"Son of the Beginning"** — the Son was encoded in the first syllable of Scripture.

### More Hidden Words
- **אש (Esh)** = **"Fire"** — the middle letters
- **ברית (Brit)** = **"Covenant"** — the outer letters
- Together: a **"covenant of fire"** hidden in the first word of the Bible

## The Alef-Tav (את)
Genesis 1:1 contains the untranslatable word **את** (*et*) — a grammatical direct object marker made of:
- **Alef** (א) — first letter of Hebrew alphabet
- **Tav** (ת) — last letter of Hebrew alphabet

In Revelation 22:13, Jesus says: *"I am the Alpha and Omega, the First and the Last."* Alpha/Omega are the Greek equivalents of Alef/Tav.

**The Son's signature was in Genesis 1:1 from the very first verse.**

## The Thread
| Genesis 1:1 | John 1:1 |
|-------------|----------|
| "In the beginning God created..." | "In the beginning was the Word..." |
| God creates by **speaking** | The Word **is** the speech of God |
| Hidden "Bar" (Son) in *Bereshit* | The Son is revealed as the Logos |
| את (Alef-Tav) — unnamed | "I am the Alpha and Omega" — named |
