# Chiastic Structure — John 1:1-18 (The Prologue)

## What is a Chiasm?
A mirror pattern (ABCBA) where the **center** reveals the author's main point. Common in Hebrew and Greek literary tradition.

## The Prologue's Chiasm

```
A  (vv. 1-5)    — The Word's relationship to God & creation
  B  (vv. 6-8)    — John the Baptist witnesses about the light
    C  (vv. 9-11)    — The true light comes into the world
      ✦ X (vv. 12-13) — CENTER: "To all who received him, he gave
                         the right to become CHILDREN OF GOD"
    C' (v. 14)       — The Word becomes flesh, enters the world
  B' (v. 15)       — John the Baptist witnesses about the Word
A' (vv. 16-18)  — The Word reveals God to us
```

## The Center Point (vv. 12-13)
> *"Yet to all who did receive him, to those who believed in his name, he gave the right to become children of God — children born not of natural descent, nor of human decision or a husband's will, but born of God."*

John starts cosmic (the Word IS God) and drives everything to one personal question: **will you receive Him?**

The center matches John's purpose statement in **John 20:31**: *"These are written that you may believe... and that by believing you may have life in his name."*

## Micro-Chiasm in John 1:1 Itself

```
A  The Word was in the beginning
  B  The Word was with God
  B' God was
A' the Word
```

## The Prologue as Overture
Colin Kruse: the prologue functions like an overture introducing an opera. It placards every major theme:
- Life (1:4)
- Light and darkness (1:5, 7-9)
- Witness (1:7-8, 15)
- World (1:10)
- Belief and unbelief (1:11-12)
- Grace and truth (1:14, 17)
