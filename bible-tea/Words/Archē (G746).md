# Archē (ἀρχή) — G746

## Definition
- **Beginning / origin** — the start point of time
- **First principle** — the foundational cause of everything
- **Ruler / authority / chief** — governing power

## The Dual Meaning
English "beginning" is flat. Greek *archē* means both **"the start"** and **"the ruling authority."** When John writes "In the *archē*," he simultaneously says:
- "In the beginning of time..."
- "In the ruling principle of all things..."

The Word wasn't just present when time started — the Word **IS** the foundational authority.

## Derivatives
The "ruler" sense lives in English words we use daily:
- **Mon-arch** — one ruler
- **An-archy** — no ruler
- **Arch-bishop** — chief bishop
- **Arch-itect** — master builder

## Key Appearances
- **John 1:1** — "In the *archē* was the Word"
- **Genesis 1:1 (LXX)** — "In the *archē* God created the heavens and the earth"
- **Colossians 1:18** — "He is the *archē*, the firstborn from among the dead"
- **Revelation 3:14** — "the *Archē* of God's creation"
- **Revelation 21:6** — "I am the *Archē* and the End"
