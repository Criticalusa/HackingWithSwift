# Pros (πρός) — G4314

## Definition
- Primary meaning: **to, toward, unto**
- With accusative case (as in John 1:1): **with, face-to-face, in the presence of**

## Why This Matters in John 1:1
English says "the Word was **with** God." Flat. The Greek *pros ton theon* implies:

1. **Intimate communion** — not proximity, but active face-to-face relationship
2. **Distinct personhood** — you can't be face-to-face with yourself
3. **Equality** — facing each other on the same level

As one scholar wrote: *"The Logos is not an attribute inhering in God, or a power emanating from him, but a person in the presence of God and turned in loving, inseparable communion toward God, and God turned equally toward him."*

## The Debate
- *Pros* with the accusative only means "with" (in the relational sense) **24 times out of 723 NT uses**
- Its primary meaning is "to" or "toward"
- Some argue John should have used *para* ("alongside") if he meant simple proximity
- The unusual usage suggests John chose *pros* deliberately for its sense of **active orientation toward**
