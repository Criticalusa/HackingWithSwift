# Logos (λόγος) — G3056

## Definition
- A spoken word / utterance
- Reason / rational principle
- The ordering logic of the universe (Stoic philosophy)
- In John's Gospel: **the personal, pre-existent, divine Word — Jesus Christ**

## Semantic Range
| Meaning | Example Usage |
|---------|---------------|
| Spoken word | Matt 12:36 — "every careless *word*" |
| Message/teaching | Luke 4:32 — "his *word* was with authority" |
| Reason/account | Matt 12:36 — "give *account*" (same word!) |
| Divine Person | John 1:1 — "the *Word* was God" |

## Hebrew Equivalent: [[Dabar (H1697)]]
In the Septuagint, *logos* translates Hebrew *dabar*. Unlike Greek where word and deed are separate concepts, Hebrew *dabar* means **word AND deed simultaneously**. When God speaks, reality obeys.

## Key Appearances
- **John 1:1** — "In the beginning was the Logos"
- **John 1:14** — "The Logos became flesh"
- **Revelation 19:13** — "His name is the Word (Logos) of God"
- **Hebrews 4:12** — "The word (logos) of God is living and active"
- **1 John 1:1** — "the Word (logos) of life"

## Cultural Bridge
John chose *logos* because it was the perfect bridge term:
- **Jews** → heard God's creative *dabar* from Genesis 1
- **Greeks** → heard the Stoic rational principle governing the cosmos
- **Both** → understood it as the foundational force behind reality

Then John dropped the bomb: **the Logos is a Person you can meet**.
