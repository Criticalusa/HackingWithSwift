# Dabar (דָּבָר) — H1697

## Definition
- Word, speech, matter, thing, deed
- "The word of the LORD" appears **261 times** in the Hebrew OT

## The Key Insight
In Hebrew, *dabar* means **word AND deed at the same time**. A word is not just sound — it's an **action**. When God speaks, things happen. This is why Genesis 1 records creation through speech: "God *said*... and it was so."

## First Mention
**Genesis 15:1** — "After these things the *word* (dabar) of the LORD came to Abram in a vision" — the first time "the word of the LORD" appears as a distinct entity visiting a person.

## Pattern Through Scripture
1. **Genesis 1:3** — God's speech creates reality
2. **Genesis 15:1** — The word of the LORD comes to Abram as a visitor
3. **1 Samuel 3:7** — "the *word* of the LORD had not yet been revealed to him"
4. **Isaiah 55:11** — "my *word*... will not return to me empty but will accomplish what I desire"
5. **Jeremiah 23:29** — "Is not my *word* like fire... and like a hammer?"

## Greek Equivalent: [[Logos (G3056)]]
The Septuagint translates *dabar* as *logos*. John 1:1 builds on this entire OT tradition — the *dabar* that spoke creation, visited Abraham, and burned like fire in the prophets is now revealed as a Person.
