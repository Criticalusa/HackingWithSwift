# John 1:1

> *"In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God."*

## Translation Comparison

| Translation | Text |

| **NASB** | "In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God." |

All major translations agree word-for-word — the "tea" is hiding **inside the Greek and Hebrew**, not between translations.

## Greek Text Breakdown

**Ἐν ἀρχῇ ἦν ὁ λόγος, καὶ ὁ λόγος ἦν πρὸς τὸν θεόν, καὶ θεὸς ἦν ὁ λόγος.**

Three clauses, three truths:
1. **The Word is eternal** — "In the beginning was the Word"
2. **The Word is relational** — "the Word was with God"
3. **The Word is divine** — "God was the Word"

### Key Words
- [[Archē (G746)]] — "beginning" / "ruler" / "first principle"
- [[Logos (G3056)]] — "word" / "reason" / "ordering principle"
- [[Pros (G4314)]] — "with" / "face-to-face" / "toward"
- **ἦν (ēn)** — imperfect tense: continuous past existence. Not "came into being" but "was already existing, continuously"
- **θεὸς (theos)** — anarthrous (no article) = qualitative. The Word has the *nature/essence* of God

### The Word Order
Greek literally reads: **"God was the Word"** — *theos* is placed first for emphasis. John front-loads deity.

## Historical & Cultural Context

John wrote ~90 AD from Ephesus. Dual audience:
- **Jewish readers** heard "In the beginning" → Genesis. They knew [[Dabar (H1697)]] as God's active, creating power. The *Memra* in Aramaic Targums already referred to God's presence/action.
- **Greek readers** heard [[Logos (G3056)]] → Stoic rational principle ordering the cosmos (Heraclitus, ~600 BC).

John's genius: **one word both audiences understood, redefined as a PERSON**.

## The "Tea"

The first word of the Bible — *Bereshit* — contains hidden Hebrew words: **Bar** (Son) + **Reshit** (Beginning) = "Son of the Beginning." See [[Genesis 1-1 — Bereshit Connection]].

## Meditate On

**Pros ton theon** — "face to face with God." The Word wasn't distant from the Father. He was turned toward Him in eternal, intimate communion. That same Word turned His face toward you.
